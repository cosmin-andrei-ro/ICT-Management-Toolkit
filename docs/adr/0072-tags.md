# ADR 0072 — Tags

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-04

## Context

`CLAUDE.md` §3 lists tags among Core's shared features. People need a way to group records across modules that no field covers, such as "audit-2026" or "end of support". The owner chose tags on the main records: tickets, configuration items, assets, applications, risks, projects and suppliers.

## Decision

- **`core_tags` per organization, `core_taggables` polymorphic,** as `CLAUDE.md` §5.2 allows for cross-cutting features. Deleting a tag removes its links through a foreign key; force-deleting a record removes its own.
- **One spelling:** names are stored in lower case with single spaces, so "Needs  Review" and "needs review" are one tag. Letters, digits, spaces, dots, dashes and underscores, up to 50 characters, at most 20 on a record.
- **Tags appear where they are used:** anyone who may change a record can add one, and existing names are suggested. Administrators rename and delete them (`core.tag.manage`).
- **Staff only (`core.tag.view`, on every IT role):** requesters do not see them, because a name such as "breach-2026" says something by itself, including on a ticket they can read.
- **The change is recorded on the record** (`tags`, old and new names), so it shows in the record's history. Changing tags needs the same right as changing the record: `update`, or `work` for tickets.
- **A tag's page** lists what carries it through `LinkedRecords::resolve`, so each module applies its own rules for what the person may see. Tags are also in the global search.

## Alternatives considered

- **A fixed, administrator-managed list:** tidier, but people stop tagging when it means asking someone. Renaming and deleting cover the mess.
- **A filter by tag on every list:** convenient, but seven lists to change. The tag's page answers "what carries this tag" first; a filter can follow if people ask.
- **Colours:** no meaning to validate, and colour alone is poor for accessibility.

## Consequences

- A person who may see a tag can see its name even if they may see nothing that carries it.
- A tag's page shows the first 100 records of each kind, without paging.
- Tags are not in the API or in exports yet.
- Renaming a tag does not rewrite past entries in record histories.
