---
title: Projects
audience: everyone
---

**Projects** lists the IT projects you can see. Project managers and team leads see every project; others see the projects they sponsor, run or work on. **My tasks** gathers your unfinished tasks from every open project, the most urgent first.

## Starting a project

Project managers and team leads click **New project** and give:

- a **name** and a **description**;
- the **type**: *New application* when the project brings an application into the company, *Infrastructure*, or *General*;
- the **project manager** and the **sponsor**;
- the planned **start** and **end** dates.

The project gets a code (PRJ-0001) and four phases: **initiation**, **planning**, **execution** and **closure**. It starts in initiation. The project manager plans each phase's dates with **Plan dates**.

## The team

Under **Team**, the project manager adds people with their role in the project, such as "Network engineer" or "Key user". Someone who still has open tasks cannot be removed; give their tasks to someone else first.

## Tasks

The project manager adds tasks under **Add a task**, with:

- the **phase** it belongs to;
- who it is **assigned to**: the manager, the sponsor or a team member;
- a **due date**.

Tasks show as a **Board** with four columns, *To do*, *In progress*, *Blocked* and *Done*, or as a **List**. Filter them by phase. The buttons on each card move it to the next or previous column. The project manager moves any task; each person moves the tasks assigned to them, from the project or from **My tasks**. Overdue tasks are marked in red.

Closed and cancelled projects stay as they ended and can no longer be changed.
