---
title: Requesting a service
audience: everyone
---

The **service catalog** lists what IT can do for you, for example a new laptop or VPN access. Each service asks exactly the questions IT needs, so your request goes straight to the right team.

## Making a request

1. Click **Request a service**.
2. Find the service: browse the list, or type in the search box.
3. Click **Request**.
4. Answer the questions. Fields marked with a red asterisk are required.
5. Add anything else IT should know, and attachments if useful.
6. Click **Send request**.

You get a service request number such as `REQ-000017`, and you follow it like any other ticket in **My tickets**.

If what you need is not in the catalog, use **Open a ticket instead** at the bottom of the catalog.

## Good to know

- Your answers are kept with the request exactly as you gave them, even if IT changes the form later.
- If a required answer is missing or not valid, the form tells you which field to fix.
