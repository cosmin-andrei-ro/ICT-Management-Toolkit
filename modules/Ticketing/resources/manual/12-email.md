---
title: Tickets by email
audience: everyone
---

If your organization has set up a support address, you can raise tickets and reply to them by email, without opening the application.

## Raising a ticket

Write to the support address from the email address of your account. The subject becomes the ticket's title, and the message its description. Attachments are added to the ticket, with the same rules as in the portal (allowed file types and size). Files that are not accepted are listed in the ticket. Small images inside the message, such as signature logos, are left out.

You get the usual confirmation email with the ticket number.

## Replying

Every email about your ticket can be answered: just reply. Write above the line **— Reply above this line —**. Your reply is added to the ticket, and the quoted earlier conversation is left out. Keep the ticket number in the subject, for example `[INC-000123]`, so the reply finds its ticket.

If the ticket is already closed, your reply opens a new ticket that mentions the old one.

## When nothing happens

Messages are read every minute or so. A message is ignored, without any answer, when:

- it comes from an address that has no account in the application;
- it is an automatic message, such as an out-of-office reply or a delivery failure;
- the mail server could not verify that it really comes from your address (when IT staff write to a ticket by email without this check, their message stays internal until they confirm it);
- you sent many messages in a short time;
- it is very large.

If your message does not become a ticket, use the portal, or ask the service desk.
