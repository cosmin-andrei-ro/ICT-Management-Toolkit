---
title: Approving changes (CAB and ECAB)
audience: everyone
---

If you sit on a **change advisory board** (CAB) or on the **emergency CAB** (ECAB), you authorize changes to IT systems before they are made. You do not need to work in the service desk to do this.

## What waits for you

You get an email when a change is submitted to your board. **Approvals** in the menu lists every change waiting for your decision, earliest window first.

Open a change to see:

- why it is needed and its risk;
- the implementation plan, the rollback plan and how it will be verified;
- the planned window, and other changes scheduled at the same time;
- the configuration items it touches, and what their failure would affect;
- the decisions already made in this round.

## Deciding

- **Approve** if the plan is sound, the rollback is realistic and the timing is acceptable. A comment is optional.
- **Reject** if it is not. A comment is required: it goes to the requester, who can fix the plan and submit again.

**Every member of the board must approve.** A single rejection rejects the change. The person who raised the change never decides on it, even if they sit on the board.

If the requester changes the plan or the window after you approved, your approval is withdrawn, and you are asked again for the new version. Your decision is recorded with your name and the time, and it cannot be changed.
