---
title: Reporting a problem and following your tickets
audience: everyone
---

Use **My tickets** to tell IT that something is broken, and to follow everything you have asked for.

## Opening a ticket

1. Click **My tickets**, then **New ticket**.
2. Choose what you need:
   - **Incident:** something that worked has stopped working, or works badly ("the printer is offline", "VPN disconnects").
   - **Service request:** you need something new. For common requests, the **Request a service** catalog is faster and asks the right questions.
3. Write a short **summary** and the **details**: what you were doing, what you expected, what happened, and any error message.
4. Answer the two questions that set the priority:
   - **Who is affected?** One person, a team or department, or many users / a critical service.
   - **How urgent is it?** It can wait, your work is slowed down, or your work is stopped.
5. Attach screenshots or documents if they help (see the limits below).
6. Click **Submit**.

You get a number such as `INC-000042` and a confirmation email.

**Attachments:** up to 5 files per message, 20 MB each. Accepted: PDF, images (PNG, JPEG, GIF, WebP), text and CSV, ZIP, Word/Excel/PowerPoint and OpenDocument files, and saved emails (`.eml`). Other types are refused, whatever their name.

## Following your tickets

**My tickets** lists your tickets, open ones first. Use **Show** to see open, closed or all tickets. Open a ticket to see:

- its **status**;
- who is handling it;
- the **conversation** with IT;
- the attachments.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| New | Received, waiting for someone to pick it up. |
| Assigned / In progress | Someone is working on it. |
| Pending | IT is waiting for **you**. Read the last message and reply. |
| Resolved | IT thinks it is solved. Confirm or reopen it. |
| Closed | Finished. |
| Cancelled | Withdrawn or not needed. |

### Replying

Write in **Reply** and click **Send**. You can attach files too. If the ticket was *Pending*, your reply automatically puts it back into IT's hands.

### When IT resolves your ticket

You get an email. Open the ticket and:

- click **Yes, it is solved** to close it; or
- tell IT what is still wrong and click **Reopen**.

If you do nothing, the ticket closes automatically after **5 days**.

### Withdrawing a ticket

While a ticket is still *New*, you can withdraw it with **Withdraw this ticket**. After that, ask IT in a reply.

## Privacy

Only you, people added to the ticket as watchers, and the IT team can see your tickets. IT staff can also write internal notes that you do not see.

## Limits

To protect the service desk from runaway scripts, one person can open at most 20 tickets per hour.
