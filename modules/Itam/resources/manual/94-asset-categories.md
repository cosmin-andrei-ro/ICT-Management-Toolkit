---
title: Asset categories
audience: administrators
---

**Administration → Asset management → Asset categories** lists the kinds of equipment and the attributes each records. Asset managers, team leads and administrators manage them.

Ten categories are created the first time someone opens the assets: laptop, desktop, monitor, phone, tablet, printer, server, network device, storage and peripheral. Change them freely. A category you delete is not created again.

- The **key** (for example `laptop`) is used by imports and cannot change after creation.
- **Attributes** are the fields of the category's assets, for example *Memory (GB)* for laptops or *IMEI* for phones. Changing them keeps the values already recorded; a removed attribute is no longer shown.
- A category in use cannot be deleted. Mark it **inactive**: its assets keep it, but it is no longer offered for new ones.
