---
title: Software licences
audience: agents
---

**Configuration & assets → Licences** shows the software the organization pays for, grouped by product, with the seats bought and the seats in use. A product using more seats than it has is marked **Over-used**.

Agents see every licence and give or release seats. Asset managers and team leads add and change licences and can show licence keys.

## Licences

**New licence**. Choose the product, or type a new product name to create it. Then:

- **Licence**: what distinguishes this purchase, for example *E3 – 2026 agreement*.
- **Counted**: **per user** (each person uses a seat), **per device** (each computer uses a seat), or **site** (no count; you can still record who uses it).
- **Seats bought**, the validity (**Valid from**, **Expires**, empty for a perpetual licence), the supplier, the contract and the cost.
- **Licence key**: stored encrypted. To change it, type the new one; to keep it, leave the field empty.

Upload the licence certificate or the invoice under **Documents** on the licence's page.

## Seats

On the licence's page, search for a person (or, for a per-device licence, a device by its asset tag) and click **Add**. **Release** frees the seat. Disposing of a device releases its seats automatically.

You can give more seats than were bought: record what is really used. The list then marks the product as over-used, so you can buy more or release seats. Only licences that have not expired count as bought.

## Licence keys

Keys are never shown in lists, exports or emails. On the licence's page, enter a code from your authenticator app and click **Show key**. Every showing is recorded in the audit trail; after five wrong codes, wait a minute.

## Import, export and reminders

**Import licences** reads a spreadsheet with one row per licence (product, licence name, model, seats, dates, supplier, cost). Keys are never imported: enter them on each licence. **Export to spreadsheet** gives every licence with the seats in use, without keys.

Every morning, asset managers and team leads receive one email listing the licences that expire in the next 60 days. Each expiry date is announced once.
