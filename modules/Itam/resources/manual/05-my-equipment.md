---
title: My equipment
audience: everyone
---

**My equipment** in the menu lists what IT has handed to you: each device with its category, its asset tag (the number on its label), its serial number and since when you have it. The menu entry appears once you have at least one device or licence.

When IT hands you something, you receive an email. Click **I received it** next to the device to confirm. **Handover protocol** opens the protocol to print or save as PDF.

**My software** lists the software licensed to you by name. Keys are never shown here; IT installs or activates the software.

If something is missing or wrong, or you no longer have a device, tell the service desk: open **My tickets** and report it.
