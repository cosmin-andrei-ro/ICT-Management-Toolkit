---
title: Setting up the BIA
audience: administrators
---

The continuity coordinator and administrators configure the method in **Administration → Business impact analysis**. The defaults are created the first time someone opens the BIA.

- **Impact categories**: the kinds of impact scored, with what each level 1–5 means. Describe the levels in your organization's terms, for example amounts for *Financial*, so everyone scores alike. A category already used is deactivated rather than deleted.
- **Time horizons**: the periods at which impact is scored, in minutes (240 = 4 hours, 1440 = 1 day). A horizon's duration cannot change once assessments use it.
- **BIA settings**: the **unacceptable impact level** (default 4), from which an interruption is unacceptable and which sets the MTPD, and how often processes are **reviewed** (default every 12 months). A new level applies to assessments approved afterwards.

Processes belong to **departments**: **Administration → Organization → Departments**.

Give the **Continuity coordinator** role in **Administration → Access → People**. Process owners need no role: naming them as owner or deputy of a process is enough.
