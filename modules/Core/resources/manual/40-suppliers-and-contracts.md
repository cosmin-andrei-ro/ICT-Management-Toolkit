---
title: Suppliers and contracts
audience: agents
---

**Suppliers** are the companies IT buys from or relies on. **Contracts** are the agreements with them: support, licences, subscriptions, leases. The application reminds you before each contract's deadline, so nothing renews or ends by surprise.

Agents see suppliers and their contacts, to know whom to call. Contracts are visible only to asset managers, team leads and administrators, because they hold prices and terms.

## Suppliers

Open **Suppliers** in the menu. Each supplier has:

- how to reach **support**: phone, email, hours, and **our customer number**, which their support desk usually asks for;
- a legal name, a tax ID (for example the CUI) and a website;
- **contacts**: the people to call, such as an account manager or an escalation engineer. Record only business details.

A supplier that you no longer use can be marked **inactive**: it stays on old records but is no longer offered for new contracts. A supplier with contracts cannot be deleted.

## Contracts

Open **Contracts** in the menu, or **New contract** on a supplier's page. A contract has:

- a **title**, the **supplier**, a **type** and the **contract number**;
- a **start** and, unless it runs without a fixed end, an **end**;
- a **notice period**: how many days before the end it must be cancelled or renegotiated;
- **renews automatically**, with the renewal term in months. When the end passes, the application moves the end date by that term;
- the **value**, its currency and how often it is billed;
- an **owner**, who receives the reminders;
- **documents**: the signed contract, annexes, the data processing agreement… Upload PDF, Office or image files.

The **deadline** is the end date minus the notice period: the last day you can still cancel or renegotiate. The list shows the nearest deadline first.

The **status** follows from the dates:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Not started | The start date is still ahead. |
| Active | Running, with no deadline near. |
| Deadline near | The first reminder has been sent: decide whether to renew, renegotiate or cancel. |
| Ended | The end date passed. |
| Terminated | It was ended early, on the date entered in **Terminated on**. |

## Reminders

Every morning the application checks the deadlines. By default it emails the owner and the asset managers **90 days** and **30 days** before each deadline. Each reminder is sent once. If you change the end date or the notice period, the countdown starts again from the new deadline. The email does not contain the value of the contract.

Your administrator can change the number of days.
