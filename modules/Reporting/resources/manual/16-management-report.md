---
title: Management overview and monthly report
audience: everyone
---

People who were given access to the **management overview** see **Management** in the menu. It brings together the figures of every module — service desk, assets, business impact analysis, continuity, applications, risks, compliance, documentation — even modules you do not work in. You see figures only, never the records behind them.

For each figure:

- **Now**: its value at this moment;
- **Status**: *On track*, *Needs attention* or *Act now*, where the figure has thresholds;
- **Change in 30 days**: how much it went up or down;
- **Last 90 days**: a small line of its daily values.

## The monthly report

Choose a month under **Monthly report** and click **Open the report**. You can pick the current month, which shows the figures so far, or any of the eleven months before. The report shows, for each figure:

- its value at the end of the month and at the end of the month before;
- its status;
- its last twelve months.

A summary at the top counts the figures by status. **Print** it, or **Download PDF** where the PDF service is installed. A past month always shows the same figures, because the report is built from the values recorded each day.

## Receiving it by email

On the first day of each month, the people an administrator chose receive the report of the month that ended. The email says how many figures need action or attention and links to the report. Where the PDF service runs, the report is also attached as a PDF.

Administrators manage the list at the bottom of the **Management** page: choose a person and click **Add**, or **Remove** someone. Only active members who may see the management overview can be added; someone who loses that access stops receiving the report.
