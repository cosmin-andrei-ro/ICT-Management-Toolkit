The home page shows a **dashboard** with the figures that need attention in the areas you work in: open tickets, licences about to expire, risks with overdue actions, controls whose test is late, and so on. You only see the figures of the modules you have access to, grouped by module.

Each figure has a colour:

- **green** (on track): nothing to do;
- **amber** (needs attention): something is late or close to it;
- **red** (act now): the situation needs action soon;
- **white**: a figure shown for information.

Click a number to open the list behind it, already filtered — for example, the open tickets without an assignee.

## Arrange it your way

Click **Customise**:

- **Hide** the figures you do not need; they stay greyed out while you customise, and **Show** brings them back;
- **Move up** and **Move down** put the groups in the order you prefer.

Click **Done** when you are finished. Your arrangement is yours alone. **Restore defaults** brings back every figure in the original order. New figures added in later versions appear on their own.
